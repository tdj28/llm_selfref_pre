# Llama Steering Pilots And Specificity

How do target interventions compare with matched features, and are their
effects specific to consciousness-report wording? These completed studies
precede or supplement the [prospective full grid](../public_sae_consciousness_gating/README.md).
They are separate designs, not additional blocks of that grid.
