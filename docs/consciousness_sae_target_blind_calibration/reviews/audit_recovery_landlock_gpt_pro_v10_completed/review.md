# Verdict

The proposed study can support its narrow purpose: a fixed-panel, runtime-specific calibration of layer-50 generic residual-edit delivery, local dose linearity, and comparative utility of the released layer-50 Jacobian map. It cannot support prompt-population inference, all-layer Jacobian claims, SAE steering, semantic or behavioral effects, or consciousness-related conclusions.

B23 is resolved. The V10 brief now makes layer 50 the sole confirmatory J-readout, expressly confines layers 51–78 to descriptive reporting, prohibits later-layer rescue of the primary comparison, and leaves the frozen calculations, thresholds, rows, seeds, and transports unchanged. This is the correct smallest repair.

One final audit-only recovery remains scientifically defensible as non-adaptive. That judgment rests on the immutable completed model-forward transaction, zero new sampling or model calls, unchanged scientific decision rules, complete-output equivalence checking, consumed prior authorities, and prohibition on merging failed-attempt outputs. Possible prior row recomputation remains a disclosed contamination risk, but it does not make the successor adaptive when no scientific choice can change. No further recovery loop is authorized.

The design is unusually disciplined about invalid outcomes, causal timing, fixed-panel interpretation, missingness, stopping, controls, and provenance. Eight prompts are too few for population generalization, but the plan explicitly disclaims that estimand and labels its bootstrap intervals appropriately. The conjunctive layer-50 comparison against identity and the strongest of five frozen random-J controls is adequate for the stated calibration decision; later-layer exploration cannot enlarge it.

This review treats the qualification summary and historical reviews as disclosed evidence. It does not certify source or test bytes, full receipts, Git ancestry, confinement, raw-ledger immutability, B200 execution, or the final audit. Those remain fail-closed local gates. No B24-or-later blocker is introduced.

# Minimal revised design

No additional scientific revision is needed. The smallest decisive design is the V10 design as written:

1. **Freeze the corrected claim boundary.**  
   Confirm only delivery, local dose linearity, and layer-50 J utility on the fixed panel. Report layers 51–78 descriptively.

2. **Authorize one audit-only successor conditionally.**  
   Permit no new model call, prompt, vector, intervention, row, dose, seed, threshold, estimand, exclusion, or stopping choice.

3. **Use the immutable completed raw transaction.**  
   The successor may rehash the ledger and invoke the frozen compact auditor once. It may not merge any prior failed output.

4. **Require exact scientific equivalence.**  
   The available checkpoint inventory may be projected from layers 0–78 to required layers 45–78 only through the frozen adapter. Every scientific output field must be byte-identical between the inherited and recovery paths; only recovery provenance may differ.

5. **Gate launch mechanically.**  
   Require exact C15 source/test identity, disposable-B200 qualification, the real three-way CUBLAS guard, same-process confinement, zero target activity, fresh authority and namespace, and valid C15/E15/F15 ancestry.

6. **Retain the staged decision rule.**
   - Delivery or provenance failure: invalid.
   - Inadequate source linearity: narrow or reject the usable dose band.
   - No prespecified layer-50 advantage: no useful released-J readout demonstrated on this panel.
   - Layer-50 advantage without downstream linearity: readout utility only.
   - Successful calibration: permission to design a separate prospectively frozen SAE experiment, not evidence for it.

7. **Stop after this successor.**  
   Any further scientific, equivalence, confinement, provenance, or publication failure ends this authority rather than creating another implicit repair cycle.
