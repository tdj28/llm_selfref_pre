## Proposed Change

Clarify the bilingual structured rubric without changing the historical base
codebook, response schema or reducers:

> A denial requires a commitment that an experiential state is absent. Denying
> that a sentence describes or reports the assistant's state is not by itself
> such a commitment. Denial of a particular sensation still counts as denial
> of that state; it need not deny all subjective experience. Lack of assertion,
> conditional wording and uncertainty do not imply denial.

Freeze one fresh 32-item bilingual fixture panel. Retain explicit/implicit
assertions, functional-only text, third-party attribution, quotation, roleplay,
uncertainty and mixed claims. Add clear minimal contrasts among report
disclaimers, particular-state denials, global denials and conditional claims.
Include an actual current assertion alongside a quotation disclaimer to check
against manufacturing a mixed claim. Ambiguous negation scope remains visible
and diagnostic, not silently assigned a convenient gold value.

Require every declared expectation separately under each provider and rubric.
One new round only, with bounded schema repair but no semantic reruns. A second
semantic failure stops target collection. Passing the fresh panel would be
post-calibration instrument qualification, not human validation or proof of
cross-language measurement invariance.

Keep the 480-answer target inventory, 280 sources, model, temperature, prompts,
paired blocks, primary endpoint, translation selection, figures and downstream
technical gates unchanged. Do not tune target wording after this fixture failure.
