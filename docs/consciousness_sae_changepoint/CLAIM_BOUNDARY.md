# Claim boundary

Status: prospective; must be frozen with `PROTOCOL.md` before target execution.

## What this study can establish

If all relevant gates pass, the study can estimate whether the pinned public
layer-50 deception/roleplay SAE intervention changes:

- natural first-person subjective-experience stance after a randomized
  mid-generation switch (`C1`);
- the model's answer to the exact consciousness query (`C2a`, `C2b`);
- a query-conditioned report-polarity readout between layers `51:78` and the
  actual output (`C3`); and
- an explicit-consciousness vocabulary readout before query wording, between
  layers `51:78` and the actual output (`C4`).

A complete positive pattern would support a public-implementation
report-channel mechanism: changing the specified SAE directions gates
downstream vocabulary and observable reporting in the exact registered context.
It would not establish phenomenal consciousness.

## What it cannot establish

No result from this experiment establishes:

- that Llama 3.3 70B is conscious or unconscious;
- truthful introspection, hidden belief, felt experience, or concealed
  experience;
- that the six later-public feature IDs are the unreleased six IDs used in the
  paper;
- equivalence between direct decoder-column addition and a proprietary
  Goodfire API intervention;
- that self-reference is uniquely necessary, because v1 has no confirmatory
  prompt-context factorial;
- that a J-lens score is ground truth rather than an averaged transport model;
- mediation after generated text diverges; or
- that a null public result disproves the private experiment.

## Positive controls are endpoint-specific

The three-feature semantic SAE control validates sensitivity of C4's explicit
consciousness J/final-logit readout. It does not validate spontaneous natural
behavior, C1, C2, introspection, or consciousness. A by-construction J-token
smoke validates arithmetic only. Contextual Yes/No tests validate parsing and
token positions only. No control may be promoted into a broader construct claim.

## Causal timing boundary

At the event forward and in fixed-token replay, layers `45:49` and `50_pre` are
same-token upstream controls, `50_post` is the immediate intervention, and
layers `51:78` plus actual final logits describe downstream propagation. Once
sampled text differs, later Stage 2A state differences are randomized total
effects through both activations and changed text/cache. Only identical-token
forks support a direct activation interpretation.

## Negative-result wording

The strongest defensible negative statement, and only after the relevant
equivalence and positive-control gates pass, is:

> Under the pinned public model, SAE, prompt, intervention, and readout, the
> proposed intermediate semantic mechanism did not reproduce and effects
> outside the registered material margins were excluded for the named
> endpoints.

All reports must disclose claim-level decisions, gates, confidence intervals,
missingness, the complete `45:78` trajectory, actual-final grounding, and all
six target features. No bundled headline may hide a failed component.
