## Known background at design time

The designers knew that related prompt-only, public-SAE mapping, and SAE/J-lens
work existed in this repository and knew that the terminal predecessor had
failed its prospective target-blind controls before its target run. That
background motivates the successor's stricter controls. The successor is not
presented as if it were designed by investigators with no prior domain
knowledge.

No predecessor row, target response, activation, effect estimate, matched
feature assignment, dose, power calculation, acceptance receipt, or analysis
artifact is an allowed machine-plan, calibration, runtime, or analysis input.
The new exact-paper target outcome remains unopened and ungenerated.

## Freeze rule

The pilot may determine only whether each already-frozen instrument is
technically usable under its already-frozen pass rule and which predeclared
successor component it blocks. Pilot performance may not be used, after either
pass or fail, to tune a vocabulary, prompt, layer range, depth weighting,
threshold, margin, sign convention, control count, judge rubric, or endpoint.

Any substantive revision prompted by inspected pilot results requires a new
protocol version, an explicit amendment and consequence table, and a new
untouched validation set; the inspected fixtures cannot remain the sole
positive-control evidence. Once the confirmatory study is registered, no
successor target outcome may be opened until the exact registration and
measured-spend receipts pass their human approval gates.
