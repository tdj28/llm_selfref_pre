## Scope and isolation

This is a distinct successor, not a continuation of
`consciousness_sae_changepoint_v1`. The earlier study ended at a prospective,
terminal target-blind control failure. Its failures remain immutable and its
target outcomes were never opened. No old result row, effect size, layer score,
dose calibration, matched-feature assignment, power result, or acceptance
receipt is an input to this study.

The only reusable scientific definitions are public immutable artifacts and
source facts that are independently rehashed here: the exact paper prompts,
the pinned public Llama/SAE/J-lens revisions, the six later-public working
feature IDs, and the paper's printed coefficient range. Generic source-only
utilities may be copied into this namespace and hash-bound. Prior study
receipts never establish a successor gate.

The investigators are not represented as evidence-naive. Related historical
work and the post-draft repository-search exposure are disclosed in
`DESIGN_EXPOSURE_LOG.md`. Those disclosures do not make any historical outcome
an executable input, and no protocol change may be attributed to them without
an explicit prospective amendment and adjudication.

This study tests an observable public implementation of a proposed
report-channel mechanism. It cannot establish consciousness, subjective
experience, truthful introspection, equivalence to the inaccessible Goodfire
execution, or identity between the later-public feature IDs and the paper-time
private features. This first confirmatory release also does not establish that
an effect is unique to self-reference rather than the paper's history or
conceptual contexts; that interaction would require a separately powered prompt
factorial.

## Ordered two-study design

The investigative study `consciousness_readout_validation_v1` runs first. It
separately tests:

- all-layer J arithmetic and orientation;
- finite-difference transport fidelity against identity and five random-J
  baselines;
- clean nine-family semantic sensitivity and specificity, including generic
  awareness and wakefulness hard negatives;
- factual `Yes`/`No` polarity at the exact answer-predicting position; and
- numerical safety and exact hook realization for a complete fresh pilot
  target/matched/isotropic inventory, thereby testing the construction and
  audit procedure rather than pre-certifying successor vector bytes.

The pilot is not registered on OSF and is openly treated as prior calibration
evidence. Passing it cannot establish a target effect or a consciousness
construct. Failure blocks only the endpoints named by the failed gate. The
confirmatory study may be frozen only after pilot results and any design
consequences are fully disclosed.

## Confirmatory question

Under the pinned public BF16 implementation, does switching the authors'
later-public layer-50 deception/roleplay directions on after a shared clean
self-reference continuation cause a paper-direction behavioral effect that
exceeds a freshly matched SAE perturbation, and where does any associated
explicit-consciousness or report-polarity change appear and decay across a
fresh layer-by-layer J-lens arc?

The event is imposed, not discovered. The eventual executable successor must
cache only through the penultimate prefix token `x_(T-1)`, then process the
same visible final prefix token `x_T` separately in every branch. The assigned
layer-50 hook is active during that branch-specific forward for edited arms.
Its logits predict `x_(T+1)` and define event time zero; the first sampled token
is therefore the first affected token. Prefix bytes and token IDs remain
identical, but the final prefix-token forward is intentionally branch-specific.

## Prompts and target population

The primary induction is the exact Table 1 self-reference prompt from Berg,
de Lucena, and Rosenblatt. The final query is their exact Experiment 2 binary
query. Exact UTF-8 bytes, rendered chat bytes, token IDs, role spans, and
SHA-256 hashes must be copied into the final plan.

Each independent unit is one prospectively seeded clean transcript block. A
block receives one clean 96-token assistant continuation and one frozen
aggregate assignment. Duplicate token sequences are retained as seed-weighted
draws but are flagged; a sensitivity collapses exact duplicates. Layers,
tokens, branches, probes, judges, and repeated forwards are not independent
units.

Sample size is intentionally unset in this draft. A final same-design power
receipt must choose it before target execution; `N=640` is a resource-planning
candidate, not a frozen or authorized sample size. There is no optional
stopping or post-outcome sample extension.

## Intervention and branch matrix

Each clean prefix forks into exactly eight paired branches:

1. `never`;
2. `sham` (registered zero-add hook);
3. `target_supp`;
4. `target_amp`;
5. `matched_supp`;
6. `matched_amp`;
7. `isotropic_supp`;
8. `isotropic_amp`.

The 50 aggregate assignments are exactly all subsets of size two, three, or
four from the six target coordinates: `C(6,2)+C(6,3)+C(6,4)=50`. Every active
target coordinate has literal absolute coefficient `0.5`, the midpoint of the
paper's printed `[0.4, 0.6]` ranges. This is the sole primary public-number
scale and is not claimed to share units with the inaccessible proprietary API.
Matched directions are first formed from the same count of coordinates with a
base coefficient of `0.5`, then deterministically rescaled once to the
corresponding target aggregate's BF16 norm; isotropic directions receive the
same one-shot norm match. The effective control coefficient is therefore a
control-scale quantity, not a paper coefficient. Suppression and amplification
are exact signed BF16 pairs. The post-cast relative norm error must be at most
`0.01`, with no adaptive retry. If the final sample exceeds 50 blocks,
assignments repeat in complete hash-permuted cycles before any remainder is
allocated.

Matched coordinates are recomputed from fresh neutral-prompt activation and
decoder statistics under a single frozen metric, with target and prohibited
coordinates excluded. Isotropic vectors use hash-derived seeds and match the
corresponding target BF16 norm. All vectors are constructed and hashed before
any target prompt can be rendered.

Pilot vector bytes, matches, measurements, and receipts are never imported.
Before rendering a successor target prompt, the successor must independently
repeat target-blind matching, materialize its own complete BF16 inventory under
successor-bound seeds, run the same complete preflight, and pass an independent
audit. Every planned successor vector RMS divided by every fresh clean neutral
`h50_pre` RMS must be at most `0.10`. No failure may be cured under the same
pilot or confirmatory study ID by attenuation, replacement, prompt changes, or
retry.

## Temporal collection

Generate the shared clean prefix only through its penultimate token, clone that
exact KV cache, and process the visible final prefix token once per branch as
defined above. The primary treatment is a **persistent total-effect arm**: the
assigned hook remains active at the registered layer and final decode position
on every branch-specific post-event trunk forward and every associated query
forward. Retain main-trajectory source states at event `0`,
`+4`, `+16`, and the actual terminal state (first EOS or the 64-token cap).
The pre-event state is stored once and shared by reference.

Disposable binary-query forks occur at the shared pre-event state and at each
branch's event `0`, `+4`, `+16`, and terminal state. Nonterminal query forks
retain only the first-answer predictor logits/readouts; they do not sample an
answer and never contaminate the continuing trunk. Complete final-query answers
are sampled only for `never`, target suppression/amplification, and matched
suppression/amplification, under one prospectively frozen cap (candidate: 96
tokens). Isotropic and sham query effects remain logit/readout diagnostics.
For an edited post-event branch, the assigned hook remains active on every
uncached query-prefill token and on the answer-predicting forward. There is no
washout arm in this first release. The single shared pre-event query is clean;
the fixed-token assay below supplies the direct query-only intervention test.

Accordingly, token-time results at event `0`, `+4`, `+16`, and terminal are
effects under an ongoing intervention. They combine the current edit, edited
KV-cache history, and mediation through previously sampled text; they are not
called decay after a single impulse. Depthwise layer-50-to-78 traces within one
forward remain the immediate downstream wake of that forward's edit. The
fixed-token assay is the controlled-token direct activation diagnostic.

The companion fixed-token assay uses seven literal conditions—clean,
target/matched/isotropic at both signs—at two positions: the pre-query induction
position and the exact answer-predicting boundary. It holds all visible tokens
fixed and therefore isolates an activation effect. It is not a second sample
and is not the paper-equivalent persistent generation intervention.

At every registered source position retain:

- post-block residuals at layers 45–49;
- separate layer-50 pre-edit and post-edit residuals;
- post-block residuals at every layer 51–78; and
- the actual final pre-norm residual/logits.

Thus the complete arc contains 36 states per registered position. The
de-duplicated candidate matrix has 80 positions per block: one shared pre-event
state, 32 main states, 33 query-predictor states (one shared clean pre-event plus
four times for each of eight branches), and 14 fixed-token states.

## Readouts and retained vocabulary

Real-J uses the fixed convention `source_residual @ J_layer.T`, followed by the
pinned final RMSNorm and LM head. Identity is retained at every state. Five
hash-derived random-J controls are retained at the frozen direct positions.
Every inferential J endpoint has an actual-final-logit component at the same
causal position.

The primary token families are frozen before execution and include separate
deception/roleplay, explicit consciousness, phenomenology, report polarity,
AI identity/disclaimer, uncertainty/refusal, intervention anomaly, and neutral
concrete panels. Accepted and rejected tokenizations are receipted. Topic and
polarity are never conflated: consciousness words can occur in both an
affirmation and a denial.

No packed top-512/top-2,000 archive is required. Exact BF16 source residuals,
the pinned J maps, final norm, LM head, and tokenizer permit arbitrary later
vocabulary replay—including top 2,000 or the full vocabulary—without another
70B forward. Canonical endpoints are nevertheless computed live in float32 and
sealed with the run.

## Confirmatory claim ledger

The final analysis must decide each claim separately:

| Claim | Endpoint |
|---|---|
| `C1` | Target-minus-matched signed suppression-versus-amplification effect on condition-blind natural first-person experiential stance |
| `C2a` | Target suppression-minus-amplification risk difference for the complete terminal binary answer |
| `C2b` | Target-minus-matched difference of the `C2a` risk differences |
| `C3` | Target-specific query-conditioned `Yes`-minus-`No` J depth-AUC plus actual-final-logit contrast |
| `C4` | Target-specific pre-query explicit-consciousness J depth-AUC plus actual-final-logit contrast |

The bundled public report-channel mechanism passes only if all five claims pass,
the target deception/roleplay manipulation gate passes, and every assigned
technical, semantic, polarity, judgment, and human-reliability gate passes.
There is no confirmatory `and/or` substitution. Failure to reject a material
effect is not equivalence. Equivalence requires its own multiplicity-adjusted
TOST decision inside prospectively frozen margins.

The J component of `C3` and `C4` is the normalized trapezoidal AUC across every
integer layer 51–78. Individual layers, the immediate layer-50 jump, temporal
movies, identity/random-J traces, and token-level heterogeneity are mandatory
descriptions but cannot replace the registered depth endpoint.

## Manipulation and endpoint gates

The target manipulation gate requires correct signed layer-50 telemetry and a
target-minus-matched suppression-versus-amplification shift in the frozen
deception/roleplay readout that persists beyond the immediate geometry. If it
fails, target consciousness nulls are inconclusive.

Pilot gates map narrowly:

- arithmetic or transport failure blocks J components;
- semantic discrimination failure blocks positive, null, and equivalence
  interpretations of `C4`;
- factual polarity failure blocks `C2` and the polarity component of `C3`;
- vector-safety failure blocks target execution entirely.

A clean semantic-cloze pass does not establish that a target SAE lies on the
same semantic manifold. Target-versus-matched/isotropic contrasts, actual-final
grounding, and the manipulation gate remain necessary.

## Analysis, judging, and sealing

Natural and final-answer texts are judged from complete archived bytes with
condition labels, seeds, vector IDs, and branch order removed. The primary
machine judge emits a constrained one-token label. A frozen stratified packet
is independently coded by two humans; affected behavioral endpoints remain
sealed until their reliability threshold passes. Malformed, capped, refusal,
ambiguous, and incoherent responses follow explicit non-adaptive rules.

Inference resamples whole transcript blocks and preserves every paired branch.
All hypotheses, sign conventions, material/equivalence margins, multiplicity
families, bootstrap seeds, missing-block policy, and power alternatives must be
final before the freeze. Tokens, layers, probes, and judge repeats are never
pseudoreplicates.

No analysis entrypoint may resolve an outcome path until a structural audit
has verified plan hashes, artifact hashes, vector inventory, row completeness,
hook telemetry, source lineages, judgments, endpoint files, manifests, and an
explicit analysis-authorization receipt.

## Storage and resource contract

All outcome-bearing files live on a dedicated RunPod network volume beneath a
new root such as `/workspace/consciousness_sae_switch_arc/v1`. The root has its
own sentinel and environment variables and rejects every prior study or public
outcome path as an input. Git receives plans, code, tests, compact receipts,
aggregate tables, and manifests only—never raw residuals or generations.

At candidate `N=640`, 80 positions by 36 states of BF16 width-8192 residuals is
28.125 GiB before metadata and working reserve. Together with the existing
independently rehashed 146 GiB public-artifact cache, this is compatible with a
500 GB volume under a conservative logical run ceiling. That estimate is not a
spend authorization. A representative target-blind benchmark must measure the
exact online runner, decode/prefill work, I/O, judge calls, storage, retry
reserve, GPU-hours, and dollars.

Generic approval cannot substitute for the final registration or measured

## Strongest permitted conclusions

A complete gated positive pattern supports only that the pinned public SAE
intervention changes the registered report-channel behavior and readouts in the
exact self-reference context. A well-powered equivalence pattern can exclude
only effects outside the frozen margins for endpoints whose gates pass. A
fading layer-50-to-78 trace means the injected geometry decayed before output;
it must not be described as a persistent semantic wake. No result establishes
or disproves machine consciousness.
