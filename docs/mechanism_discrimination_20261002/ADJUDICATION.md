## Repairs Still Needed In Pro's Proposed Design

1. **Instructed misreporting is also a compliance task.** If suppression makes
   the model ignore the inversion instruction, that is compatible with less
   instruction compliance, changed negation processing, retrieval or a
   truth-favoring answer policy. Include or explicitly defer an evidence-matched
   neutral response-transformation control before making a stronger claim.
   Separately asking for accurate and inaccurate reports does not eliminate
   this alternative by itself. Straight context retrieval is not introspection.
2. **Do not convert technical loss into hidden selection.** Pro suggests
   invalidating affected paired blocks. Instead retain every planned block,
   failure reason and partial observation; predefine worst-case sensitivity
   and the conditions under which paired-complete inference is justified.
   Counting malformed/refused objective answers as task failures is legitimate
   for that specified accuracy endpoint, not an instruction to label missing
   experience reports as denials.
3. **The proposed campaign is not a tiny pilot.** Pro proposes 256 blocks x
   2 truths x 2 instructions x 5 arms = 5,120 objective responses, plus an
   optional 128 x 5 = 640 experience responses. The total is 5,760 before
   qualification, not a costed or funded design. One-token answers can be
   cheap, but prefills, hook overhead and batching must be measured. Its
   SD=0.65 planning calculation gives approximately 0.114 minimum detectable
   effect at 80% power; that assumption and corroboration-family power are
   unverified. The proposed +0.15 threshold is a design judgment, not a fact.
4. **No unconditional second repair or repeated search.** The suggested one
   repair allowance is not authorization. Existing failed diagnostics must
   first be understood; do not repeatedly alter the operator/task until a
   preferred behavioral effect appears. Exposure thresholds must match the
   declared operator and claim rather than become arbitrary new blockers.
5. **Keep sufficient evidence for delivered-edit audits.** A compact review
   packet is appropriate. That is not permission to discard required raw
   intervention witnesses. Define a bounded artifact inventory before a run.
