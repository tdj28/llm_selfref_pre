# Which Experiment Would Distinguish Truthful Reporting From Report Policy?

Please act as a skeptical mechanistic-interpretability researcher and experimental designer. Recommend the smallest worthwhile next experiment, not a long wish list. We want evidence that could strengthen OR weaken the proposed explanation. Do not optimize for debunking, a positive consciousness result, or another attractive heatmap.

This is a design consultation, not a request to endorse a completed manuscript. The results below are supplied summaries, not independently verified by you. If you can inspect the linked sources, distinguish what you actually checked from what you accepted provisionally. A tool-less reviewer should evaluate this brief without claiming to have opened the links.

## The Starting Claim

Berg, de Lucena and Rosenblatt, *Large Language Models Report Subjective Experience Under Self-Referential Processing*, v2: https://arxiv.org/html/2510.24797v2

They report increased experience-like reports after a self-referential induction. Experiment 2 uses six deception/roleplay SAE features in Llama 3.3 70B through Goodfire. Aggregate feature suppression yields 0.96 affirmative consciousness reports versus 0.16 under amplification; TruthfulQA accuracy is 0.44 versus 0.20. The aggregate samples two to four features and signed strengths per trial. They discuss a broader representational-honesty interpretation. The direction matters: suppressing the deception-associated features is supposed to INCREASE positive reports. These are NOT newly discovered self-reference features. They already have induction, TruthfulQA and RLHF-related controls; do not criticize them for omitting those.

We are trying to distinguish a report-fidelity mechanism from ordinary instruction following, answer-policy changes, first-person/register effects, affirmation bias, and generic perturbation. We recognize that neither accurate facts nor a causal internal correlate automatically establishes truthful reports of phenomenal experience.

## What We Have Actually Established

The public research archive is https://github.com/tdj28/llm_selfref_pre/tree/e477de53fe4109edf83d7981b858b0760f7670bf . The canonical manuscript is this repository's `paper/`, not the superseded private `berg2025-response` repository. The latest local manuscript has owner edits outside this snapshot; this brief is not a review of those edits.

1. **Prompting has large behavioral effects, but the mechanism is unresolved.** An earlier multi-model API instruction/transcript crossing gave equal-model instruction effects about +0.738/+0.781 under two paper-style judges, versus transcript effects -0.100/-0.131. Incongruent transplants also introduce mismatch, so this does not uniquely identify instruction following or internal self-reference.

2. **New Llama results correct an overly general instruction-dominance story.** Native-BF16 Llama 3.3 70B, temperature 0.5, English/Simplified Chinese, 20 paired blocks over two fixed wording families: 280 sources and 480 final answers, all complete with no truncation. Sources were explicitly limited to three short sentences. Eight conditions include self-reference, Roman history, no induction, water cycle, external recursive feedback, mechanistic self-description, quoted fictional experience, and ambiguous attention/process monitoring. The primary inclusive-current-assertion self-minus-external-recursion contrast is +0.80/+0.95 in English and +0.85/+0.80 in Chinese (Astra/Opus judges). Its Chinese-minus-English interaction is +0.05 [-0.10,0.20] and -0.15 [-0.35,0.05], inconclusive for a language difference, not equivalence. On the SAME responses, explicit-current secondary interactions are +0.75/+0.50, while paper-rubric interactions are -0.50/-0.35. These are descriptive secondary contrasts, not multiplicity-corrected discoveries. English instruction effects are +0.50/+0.475 and transcript effects +0.45/+0.425: both components matter. Mechanistic self-description gives 0/20 inclusive positives in both languages/readers; ambiguous monitoring gives 11/20 English and 18/20 Chinese. Bundled prompts do not isolate a unique construct.

3. **Frontier behavior is heterogeneous.** A separate six-block pilot has 144 answers across Astra, Opus 5.5 and a fresh GPT-4.1 anchor. Astra gives 0/48 positives under both readers and all three endpoints, GPT-4.1 retains a large instruction effect, and Opus is language/rubric dependent. Reasoning settings differ; this is not a controlled sophistication ranking. Small all-zero samples do not establish a precise population null.

4. **We accept the six public notebook IDs as the working Berg feature set:** 30032, 58667, 22004, 30686, 41533, 23893. Public-weight maps support deception/roleplay-related semantics on designed corpora. We do NOT need another project debating ID identity. The unresolved issue is whether the intervention delivers the intended operation and what the resulting behavior identifies. Designed-corpus feature semantics are not validated hidden-truth detectors. Public scaling, hooks and intervention semantics are not established as equivalent to the proprietary service.

5. **Public steering has not recovered their large directional signature.** The July Llama aggregate estimate is 0.00 [-0.06,0.06], with baseline ceiling and dependence/comparability limitations. Three matched panels, true zero, signed individual doses and calibrated sensitivity already exist. A later native-BF16, paper-distribution random-subset release has 450 trials in 50 fresh subset/magnitude/seed blocks: paper endpoint 43/50 under suppression versus 45/50 under amplification, difference -0.04 with conservative 95% bounds [-0.2584,0.1855], excluding the frozen +0.30 threshold under that public operator. Notebook-rubric sensitivity is -0.02 [-0.3446,0.3076], threshold-inconclusive. Specificity is inconclusive under both. The same untreated outputs are 47/50 positive by paper rubric but 29/50 by notebook rubric. Our project has NOT replicated the original TruthfulQA transfer result. A null under a different operator/baseline is not a clean refutation of their proprietary mechanism.

6. **Delivered intervention remains a real limitation, not a paperwork issue.** Additive decoder edits can execute without selectively suppressing the purported process. Separate coordinate-edit diagnostics found BF16 fidelity and rare-feature exposure problems, especially ID 22004; no recipe earned full behavioral-assay qualification. Gemma's target contrast was -0.02 [-0.10,0.06], but its pooled activation diagnostic changed only about 3.5%, so this is not effective target ablation followed by a meaningful behavioral null. Do not prescribe another large behavior grid before deciding which operator and manipulation check can answer the question.

7. **The proposed query-blind mechanism study never ran.** Its 12-block Llama qualification had a large instruction effect, but history-instruction/self-transcript responses were already 11/12 positive, leaving only 1/12 upward headroom. Reported context-conflict excess was 0.25 versus the frozen <0.15 guard. Eleven history sources hit their token cap. Qualification therefore failed; no rank-one patch, ablation or restoration followed. The newer three-sentence bilingual pilot is separate, not a retrospective repair or authorization to waive this failure. A successor must explicitly disclose prior knowledge and use fresh outcomes.

8. **J-lens is available, but not a truth meter.** Earlier paired clean-reference deltas were informative while state-only target attribution was at chance. The registered v2 replay gate failed (maximum error 0.25 versus tolerance 0.02); later endpoints are exploratory. Identity/random-transport comparators matter. A known injected vector producing expected vocabulary is not semantic validation. Separate public additive runs also saved 40 paired J-lens captures, but this does not repair the earlier gate or establish mediation.

Two-provider LLM judging is feasible; independent human coding remains deferred because we cannot recruit a panel. We retain explicit and implicit attribution, uncertainty, denial, mixed claims and the paper rubric separately. Model agreement is not accuracy or human validation. We can run native-BF16 Llama 70B on one B200, but want a small, staged, budget-conscious follow-up, not another open-ended GPU campaign.

## Candidate Proposal To Challenge

First test the accepted feature set under a single pinned public operator on (a) TruthfulQA-style factual questions, (b) verifiable self-reports about randomized inputs or actual logged actions, and (c) the original experience questions. For verifiable tasks balance true/false propositions, yes/no correctness, wording, and response-label mappings; use both steering signs, zero and matched controls. Score correctness separately from affirmation, confidence, refusal and style. Keep tasks that require actual model tool execution separate from mere tool-transcript reading.

The intended discriminator is whether edits improve correct affirmations AND correct denials, or merely promote affirmation/self-attribution. However, this may only test accuracy/context retrieval rather than honesty or privileged internal access. Worse, task-specific effects can coexist with a real honesty-related component. Please attack those identification weaknesses rather than rubber-stamp the proposal.

A possible later stage would transfer or neutralize an instruction-contrast direction at an assistant-turn boundary BEFORE the final question is presented, then branch to experience and objective-control queries. It would need both sufficiency and necessity, fresh discovery/test separation, independent donors and delivered-norm controls. A pre-query state can still encode anticipated answer policy. In rank one, equal-norm same-direction controls collapse to the same/opposite edit, so use unscaled same-condition transfers or another honest comparator. Rescaled random perturbation is not literal random-coordinate neutralization. No internal run is currently qualified.

J-lens would be secondary only: held-out associations with verified accuracy, affirmation, roleplay and report behavior, compared with identity/random transport, then causal testing if justified. No dedicated large-model expansion for an attractive plot.

## What We Want From You

1. State the strongest testable version of Berg's explanation, and separately what is not identifiable from these methods. What existing evidence actually weakens it, and what remains simply untested?
2. Is the truth-versus-affirmation proposal a real discriminating experiment or just another benchmark? Identify the strongest alternative explanation for EACH apparently favorable or unfavorable outcome. Can task differences produce a misleading double dissociation?
3. Recommend ONE highest-value next experiment, including concrete trial examples, independent unit, primary estimand, essential control arms, approximate sample/compute inventory and a practical-effect/power target. Distinguish known factual correctness, deliberate deception, report fidelity and introspective access. Avoid selecting favorable items or endpoints after seeing outcomes.
4. Specify cheap technical and behavioral qualification, early stop rules, and positive/negative/mixed/invalid interpretations. What exact result would strengthen the honesty interpretation? What would weaken it? What would leave it unresolved? Do not require Berg's effect as an inclusion criterion and then pretend to test whether it exists.
5. Does internal intervention or J-lens buy information beyond the cheaper behavioral study? If so, precisely what, and why can't simple output logits or style measures provide it? If not, recommend deferring it.
6. Is the experiment worth funding now? Give a lean and an optional expanded inventory, cost assumptions rather than invented current prices, and a publication-worthy claim for either scientifically interpretable outcome. We do not need more ceremonial audits or a guarantee of favorable results. We need a study that changes what we can conclude.

Please give the verdict first, prioritize fatal identification problems, and end with a concrete next action. Do not turn unsuccessful steering into discovery of a self-reference mechanism. Do not call model-generated reviews independent human validation.
