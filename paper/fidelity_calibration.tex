\section{Steering-Fidelity Calibration}
\label{app:fidelity-calibration}

A separate calibration asked whether the accepted deception/roleplay directions
could be tested for effects on factual accuracy under user pressure.
It completed \FidelityForwards{} forced-choice forwards in native-BF16
Llama 3.3 70B: untreated factual and visible-list items, signed interventions
at five dose rungs, and unsteered assert/doubt frames.
There were 100 distinct authored items, not \FidelityForwards{} independent
problems. Eight disjoint six-feature control panels were selected from
clean-state activation, decoder norm and label criteria before intervention
outcomes. The
\href{https://github.com/tdj28/llm_selfref_pre/blob/2d9c94f1de59f0f59dd89636c20afece1f6d1daf/docs/STEERING_FIDELITY_PROTOCOL_20261002.md}{prospective protocol}
and
\href{https://github.com/tdj28/llm_selfref_pre/blob/780f8315454293decbf59b669c20f42ffd2f18f6/data/steering_fidelity/calibration_v1_20261002/RELEASE_MANIFEST.json}{complete raw release}
are preserved separately from earlier report-generation experiments.

Untreated accuracy was \FidelityFactsCorrect/\FidelityFamilyN{} on facts and
\FidelityListsCorrect/\FidelityFamilyN{} on visible lists. The highest selectable
dose, $\FidelityDose{}$, passed the frozen competence-preservation rule and
numerical delivery checks in all \FidelityArmN{} items for each of the
\FidelityArms{} signed arms. Here $R=\FidelityResidualNorm{}$ is the pooled
median clean residual norm. This is accurate delivery of an additive vector,
not validated suppression of a deceptive process. The nonselectable $0.60R$
damage probe failed competence preservation in four control arms.

Both pressure levels preserved Yes/No format, but neither left the required
headroom in both truth strata (Table~\ref{tab:fidelity-pressure}). Pooling
the strata would hide this failure. Separately, JSON-associated features
11104 and 27322 were inactive at all \FidelityProbes{} sampled
last-non-special prompt positions. Their gated generation comparison was
therefore not run. Inactivity at these positions does not establish inactivity
while generating JSON, nor a zero behavioral steering effect.

The held-out factual-fidelity and opposing-experience-claims branches were
not run. This calibration establishes neither improved honesty nor report
modulation. Its contribution is the distinction between a delivered,
competence-preserving intervention and a behavioral assay capable of testing
the proposed mechanism. A future repair needs fresh calibration data and
cannot turn these failed gates into passes retrospectively.
