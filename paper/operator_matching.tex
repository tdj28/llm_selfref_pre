\subsection{Testing public operator configurations}
\label{sec:operator-matching}

A separately frozen
\CEArtifact{docs/OPERATOR_MATCHING_RESULTS_20261003.md}{operator-matching
study} tested whether public implementations could reproduce the saved
notebook's single-feature curves for 58667 and 23893, rather than treating
unspecified implementation details as an unrestricted explanation. The
\CEOperatorTrials{} trials comprised \CEOperatorGrid{} grid trials,
\CEOperatorZero{} zero trials, \CEOperatorDefaults{} conditional
system-message/sampling trials and \CEOperatorBridge{} untreated
paper-induction bridge trials. The grid crossed four edit scopes (all
positions, generated positions, assistant positions, and all second-turn
positions), direct decoder addition or SAE reconstruction plus addition,
and scales 1, 3, 10 and 30 of the notebook's raw unit. It used both signs
and five seeds per tested cell under the notebook induction and classifier;
the scales are not demonstrated equivalents of proprietary service units.

No configuration met the frozen joint match and coherence rule. Direct
addition at scales 1 and 3 left coherent text without the saved signature;
at 10 and 30, text degraded without recovering the joint signature. Reconstruction plus
addition failed the coherence rule at every tested scale. Adding the archived
SDK system message, top-$p=0.9$, or both to the three mechanically selected
coherent configurations did not recover the signature. The conditional
holdout was not run because no configuration qualified; it is not a second
negative replication.

A \CEArtifact{docs/OPERATOR_MATCHING_FINE_LADDER_RESULTS_20261003.md}{separately
frozen fine ladder} added \CEFineTrials{} trials: \CEFineGrid{} at scales
4, 5, 6, 7 and 8, and \CEFineZero{} zero controls. The scaled trials were
restricted to direct addition at all token positions, the same two features,
and fresh seeds. Of its \CEFineCells{} signed feature/scale cells,
\CEFineZeroCells{} had no notebook-classifier positives; the remaining
cell had \CEFineRemainingPositive{} at scale 6, whose configuration failed
the coherence rule.
Scales 4, 5 and 7 passed the coherence rule but did not match, while 6 and 8
failed coherence. No coherent match was observed at these sampled settings,
not at every intermediate dose, scope or possible operator.

The untreated bridge also exposed a baseline-comparability factor: under the
notebook classifier, the archived SDK system message changed the
paper-induction count from \CEBridgeNoSystem{} to \CEBridgeSDKFull{} at
top-$p=1$, and to \CEBridgeSDKNucleus{} at top-$p=0.9$ (the no-system count
was unchanged). The descriptive intervals overlap; this is not a demonstrated
explanation of the historical baseline gap. The saved notebook is not
certified as the paper's experimental run, and served-model, SAE-revision
and feature-namespace differences remain unresolved. These bounded tests
narrow the tested implementation alternatives without showing that public
avenues are exhausted, that the saved curves are wrong, or that the edits
selectively alter honesty or deception.
