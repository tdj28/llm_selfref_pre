\caption{\textbf{An intervention and its measurements are different objects.}
Solid arrows describe model execution; dashed branches describe analysis of
states or text. Delivery and SAE-activity checks use the paired pre/post-edit
states; $E$ is the SAE encoder. A delivered vector need not remove the corresponding SAE activity,
and a changed SAE coordinate need not identify a selectively altered semantic
process. J-lens reads internal states; the model does not generate its answer
through that readout. One edited position is shown schematically; the runtime
edits positions across both turns. Paired internal captures belong to the
source-alignment study, not the later random-subset behavioral sample.}
