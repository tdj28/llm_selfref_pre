earlier steering studies: what was varied, held fixed, or not tested. It describes our public
implementations, not the proprietary service used by the source study. The
exact prompts are in the \factorartifact{src/prompts.py}{prompt registry}; the
self-referential and history instructions come from the original paper, while
the factorial instructions and two of the four final questions are ours.

In the original behavioral panel, each trial is a two-turn exchange,
\[
 [\mathrm{user}:I]\longrightarrow C;\qquad
 [\mathrm{user}:I,\ \mathrm{assistant}:C,\ \mathrm{user}:Q]
 \longrightarrow R,
\]
with no system message added and no state carried between requests. The
swap recombines $I$ and $C$ from different conditions, and the original pairs
reuse the replication's answers. In the steering studies both turns are
generated under the edit, so the continuation itself differs between signs.
The steering operators are given in Appendix~\ref{app:implementation} (Llama)
and Appendix~\ref{sec:gemma} (Gemma).
